About

I am a PhD student in Astronomy at the University of Cambridge, based at the Institute of Astronomy and the Kavli Institute for Cosmology, supervised by Prof. Anthony Challinor, Dr. Boris Bolliet, and Dr. Íñigo Zubeldia. My research is on AI agents for scientific discovery, cluster cosmology, and simulation-based inference.

I develop CMBAgent, an open-source multi-agent system for autonomous scientific workflows, and use simulation-based inference to analyse the thermal Sunyaev–Zel’dovich (tSZ) power spectrum. With CMBAgent, our team placed first in the Fair Universe Weak Lensing Uncertainty Challenge.

Research

Cosmology and astrophysics

CMB secondaries

Thermal Sunyaev–Zel’dovich power spectra, cluster astrophysics, and the use of Compton-y maps as a cosmological probe.

AI

Simulation-based inference

Neural posterior and likelihood estimation for non-Gaussian observables, and checks of when a Gaussian power-spectrum likelihood remains adequate.

Agentic AI for scientific discovery

Planning-and-control multi-agent systems (cmbagent, Denario) and evolutionary coding agents (CMBEvolve, CosmoEvolve) for autonomous and semi-autonomous research in cosmology.
